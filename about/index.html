Our mission

Help people protect what matters.

Homes and properties are personal. When something breaks, leaks, or needs attention, you deserve help that’s quick, honest, and skilled. Handy Angels exists to bring local pros together so customers don’t have to chase multiple contractors for every need.

Clear communication and practical solutions.

We keep it straightforward: what’s happening, what it takes to fix it, and what comes next. Whether it’s a one-time repair or ongoing maintenance, we aim for dependable work and peace of mind.

Our Team

Meet the Handy Angels Team

Handy Angels brings together trusted local professionals with real experience, honest communication, and a shared commitment to helping homeowners, rentals, and properties across the Coachella Valley.

Owner

Mike Colby

Mike Colby leads Handy Angels with a commitment to honest work, dependable service, and practical solutions for property owners across the Coachella Valley.

As the owner of Handy Angels, Mike brings together trusted professionals from multiple trades under one reliable brand so customers can get the help they need without chasing multiple contractors.

His focus is simple: fast response, clear communication, and quality work that helps people protect what matters most.

HVAC Partner

Jeff Sproul

With over 30 years of experience in the HVAC trade, Jeff Sproul is a dedicated and highly skilled certified HVAC technician known for hard work, continued learning, and reliable service.

He holds certifications from Mitsubishi, Daikin, and American Standard, and is trusted for his strong work ethic, technical skill, and friendly attitude.

Pool Partner

Tommy Caban

Tommy Caban of Got Your Six Pools has been in business since 2010 and is known for his overall knowledge of the entire pool system, honest service, and competitive bids.

He brings years of hands-on experience, dependable support, and a reputation for doing quality work with integrity.

Team Member

Mario Tapia

With over 15 years of hands-on experience in the trades and more than five years as part of the Handy Angels team, Mario Tapia is a reliable and skilled professional known for getting the job done right.

Mario has worked on a wide range of residential projects, bringing strong technical knowledge, problem-solving ability, and attention to detail to every job. Whether it’s repairs, maintenance, or upgrades, he approaches each task with a practical mindset and a commitment to quality work. When off the clock, Mario is spending time with his wife and children.

Customers appreciate Mario for his consistency, professionalism, and the confidence he brings to every project. His experience and dedication make him a trusted part of the Handy Angels team.

Team Member

Jim Padilla

Jim Padilla is the owner of Padilla Landscaping and a trusted Handy Angels partner, bringing over 25 years of experience in landscaping and outdoor property care.

With decades of hands-on knowledge, Jim is known for delivering dependable, high-quality work across a wide range of landscaping services. From routine maintenance to larger outdoor projects, he takes pride in creating and maintaining spaces that homeowners can enjoy and rely on.

As a dedicated family man, Jim brings the same values of integrity, consistency, and care into his work. His experience, reliability, and commitment to doing things the right way make him a valuable part of the Handy Angels team and a trusted partner for outdoor services.
