“You really did treat this job like it was your own home.”

A customer letter recognizing Mike Colby and Handy Angels for coordinating multiple subcontractors, completing work quickly, noticing additional items like the roof, and showing patience during a stressful time.

Hi, Mike,

The work is now done. I look forward to getting my eyes on everything on my next trip.

I want to thank you for getting it all done and in a very timely manner by coordinating with multiple subcontractors. But, I also want to recognize two specific items. First, you worked on the items we discussed when I was there. And then, you went beyond. You found other things that needed attention, like the roof, and you just got them done.
